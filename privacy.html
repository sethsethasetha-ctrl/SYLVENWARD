Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

SYLVENWARD is an offline game by yunpeng. The game does not ask for an account and does not collect personal information through its own gameplay code.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences locally on your device. This includes saved runs, best score and wave, Spirit Essence and Spirit Roots upgrades, tutorial progress, and sound, music, haptic, and screen shake settings. These values support continuing a run and remembering your choices. They are not sent to yunpeng by the game.

Network services and third parties

The game does not use an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or in-app tracking in the current version. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. Apple may process information related to downloading the app and operating the App Store under Apple's own privacy policies.

Data sharing

yunpeng does not sell or share personal information collected by the game. The game does not transmit its locally saved progress to yunpeng.

Your choices

You can change audio and other available game settings in the app. You can remove locally saved game data by deleting the app. Device backups controlled by your operating system may retain a copy according to your own backup settings.

Children

The game is not designed to collect personal information from children. No account or personal profile is required to play.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date will change. Please review the latest version when you update the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the SYLVENWARD App Store listing.
